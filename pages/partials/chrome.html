Aventurine

Aventurine is a paid app and needs to be activated before use.

  1. Open in your browser.
  2. Enter the license key from your purchase receipt to get an unlock key.
  3. Paste the unlock key below.

Merchant Icons
Shows a merchant's logo next to its name in the transactions list and the recurring calendar, for the merchants Aventurine recognizes. Turn this off to show colored initials for every merchant instead. The logos are bundled with the app; nothing is ever downloaded.

Scales the entire interface. Also adjustable with Ctrl/Cmd + Plus, Minus, and 0.
100%
Automatic Categorization
Remembers the category you assign to a description and applies it automatically to matching transactions you add or import later — including near-identical descriptions with typos or minor wording differences. Only confident matches are categorized; anything uncertain is left alone for you to review.
Delete All Transactions
Permanently removes every transaction from this database. This cannot be undone. Nothing else is affected — your categories, learned category rules, balance sheet, and all other settings are left untouched.

Hide Cents
Rounds displayed amounts to whole units for a cleaner view. Your stored values keep their cents — editing a cell still shows them.
Negative Numbers
How negative amounts are shown: a minus sign (-$1,234) or accounting parentheses (($1,234)).

How dates are written across the app (e.g. in the transactions list).
